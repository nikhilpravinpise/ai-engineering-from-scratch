Here's everything I could piece together about DJS-S4DS (the Society for Data Science student chapter at Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering, Mumbai) and the events it has run.

**Background**
S4DS itself is a parent non-profit professional association founded in 2017 to bring together industry, academia, and government labs around data science. The DJSCE student chapter was formed by Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) students in association with S4DS starting in the 2021-22 academic year, with the goal of giving members a learning platform beyond the curriculum and chances to interact with industry, participate in technical/non-technical events, and present research.

**Flagship hackathons (the "Datathon" lineage)**
This is the chapter's signature event series, run annually:
- *Datathon 1.0* – the first edition, an early hackathon with data science at its core.
- *DataHack 1.0* – a hybrid (online + offline) 24-hour datathon that drew over 950 teams from across India.
- *DataHack 2.0* (28–29 Oct 2023) – a 24-hour datathon themed around "retro games" that pulled in more than 1500 registrations and offered a prize pool of ₹1.6 lakh, with 330 participants in the final event.
- *DataHack 3.0* (19–20 Oct 2024) – a 24-hour hackathon that drew over 1,300 team registrations, of which only 40 teams were shortlisted for the final offline coding round at DJSCE. Problem statements covered flight-profile generation for aviation (Data Science track), an MLOps pipeline for a fictional restaurant chain, an adaptive multimodal-flashcard quiz platform (AI track), and an AI-driven cybersecurity assessment bot (NLP track), with three of the four tracks sponsored by companies (DrillDown, AI4EducationLabs, Allied Digital Services). Judges came from Morgan Stanley, JP Morgan, Swiggy, and others, and the event was opened by the principal, vice principal, HoD, and a chief guest.
- *DataHack 4.0: "Into the Data-Verse"* – the most recent edition, Spider-Man themed ("with great data comes great responsibility"), listed on Unstop around early 2026.

**X-Tract series**
A shorter-format sister hackathon to DataHack:
- *X-Tract* (original edition, referenced in college banners as "X-tract") and *X-Tract – A Datathon with a Story to Tell*.
- *Xtract 3.0* (5 Mar 2025) – a 6-hour hackathon run jointly with DJS Compute, covering Data Science, Machine Learning, NLP, and OpenCV, with a QR-code-based mechanic where teams scanned codes to unlock new tasks, datasets, or constraints. 84 participants competed for a ₹25,000 total prize pool.

**SkyJack**
Billed as "A Data Science Treasure Hunt" — a gamified scavenger-hunt-style event blending data science puzzles with a treasure-hunt format (it even has its own dedicated mini-site).

**Workshops & technical seminars**
- *Byte Beginnings* (24 Sept 2024) – a guidance seminar for first-years, with senior core members covering Machine Learning, Web Dev, Competitive Programming, and a dedicated "Women in Tech" segment; 115 participants attended.
- *Git-Ready / "Github and Hackathon workshop"* (21 Aug 2024) – run jointly with DJS Compute, teaching Git/GitHub basics plus hackathon prep and mindset; 81 attendees.
- *Advanced Computing with CUDA C/C++* (10 Feb 2025) – a guest session on GPU architecture, parallel computing, and CUDA programming tied to the High-Performance Computing course, with hands-on exercises.
- *MERN Stack Webinar* (8–9 Mar 2025) – a 2-day online workshop led by full-stack developer Subin S K, building a Todo-list CRUD app with MongoDB/Express/React/Node and Postman; 37 participants.
- *Intro to Compute & AppDev Workshop* and *Into the Flutterverse* (an AppDev/Flutter-focused workshop) — referenced among the chapter's homepage banner images.
- *Workshop on Machine Learning Pipeline Introduction*, *Pre-event Workshop on ML Analytics*, and *Pre-event Seminar on Statistics for DS* — preparatory sessions tied to the hackathon season.
- *Seminar on Software Development Life Cycle* — another banner-listed session.

**Webinars / expert talks**
- *Masters 101: Your Roadmap to Excellence* (10 Oct 2024) – grad-school application webinar by ex-chairperson Arihant Sheth (now at Carnegie Mellon), covering SOPs, recommendation letters, and profile-building; 99 participants.
- *S4DS Members' Exclusive Webinar – "Elevate Your AI Skills"* (23 Oct 2024) – on Prompt Engineering and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), by AI expert Dhruv Awasthi, with a live LangChain coding demo; 77 participants.
- *Expert Session on ML & DS*, *Expert Session on NLP*, and *Expert Session on Recommendation Systems* — early expert-talk series shown on the department's homepage banners.
- *TuringTested* — an AI-themed session/event (referenced in a chapter LinkedIn post tagged #DJS4DS #AI), though I couldn't pull full details on its content beyond the title.

**Internal competitions & inauguration**
- *Inauguration of DJS-S4DS / "Intro to DS"* — the chapter's founding/launch event.
- *Race4Ace* — listed among the chapter's banner-image events (an internal contest, name suggests a fast-paced coding/data challenge).
- *S4DS' Co-Code* (3 Feb 2025) – an internal coding competition at DJSCE; a team of Aishwarya Deshmukh, Akshat Singh, Harshil Bhanushali and Jay Guri won 1st place with a ₹3,000 cash prize.
- *DJS Sanshodhan – Project Poster Competition* (27 Mar 2025) – open to 2nd, 3rd, and final-year students to showcase major/IPD/hackathon/personal projects, judged on research depth, design, methodology, presentation, and impact; 45 participants, with prizes going to an AI-driven legal argument generator, a chemical-compound construction project, and a last-minute study platform.
